Specification — what a design decision is worth per stall

Recommendation

Do not pursue a 10-acre greenfield build at $175,000 per acre.

  1. Specify the hybrid section first. Paved aisles with compacted millings stalls is worth $3,988 per stall, costs nothing but a design decision, and shrinks the stormwater basin as a side effect.
  2. Then buy site size. 14+ acres at or below $120k/acre yielding 25+ stalls per gross acre is the only configuration that clears a lender's test on its own economics — 1.67× DSCR at 9.43% yield on cost.
  3. Spend the first two dollars on diligence, not land. A rate survey of secured 53 ft stalls within 15 miles, and a zoning pre-application meeting with Bloomfield TPZ. Both are cheap and either can kill the deal before land is optioned.

Two caveats sit outside the model. The $315–340/month rate is inferred from the national $150–500 band, not an observed Bloomfield comp. And Bloomfield TPZ has treated outdoor storage as a use not permitted as a principal use — a text amendment or variance is likely required, not just site-plan approval.
